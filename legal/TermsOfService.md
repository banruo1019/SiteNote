## 3. What SiteNotes does

SiteNotes is a local-first construction site logging tool. The current version provides:

- Text, photo, and voice-note capture tied to a site or project
- On-device speech-to-text transcription of voice notes
- Optional location capture (one fix per record) for site detection and weather lookup
- Daily site diary, inspection templates, and structured log entries
- PDF export of site reports and diaries
- iCloud sync of your data through your own Apple ID

SiteNotes is a tool to help you record work. It is not a substitute for professional judgement, regulatory compliance systems, or formal record-keeping required by contract or law.
