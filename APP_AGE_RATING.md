## Apple's recommendation banner

Filling these answers in App Store Connect will produce a **4+ (None)** rating for all regions.

## Notes for App Review

- The app does not target children but is suitable for everyone (4+).
- Voice transcription is local; users record their own work logs. No external content is fetched and presented to users.

## Korean broadcaster requirement
This app does not contain video / live-streamed / web-based content, so the South Korean Game-of-Chance / Broadcast questions do not apply.
